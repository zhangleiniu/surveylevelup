# Decisions

Dated structural decisions, newest first. **Append-only.** A decision that is
later overturned is not deleted — it is marked superseded, with a pointer to the
one that replaced it. That record is what stops a settled question from being
reopened as a fresh idea three sessions later.

**Does not hold:** enduring rules. When a decision hardens into a standing rule,
promote it to `CONSTITUTION.md` and leave a one-line pointer here.

Format:

```
## YYYY-MM-DD — <what was decided>
**Decision.** One or two sentences.
**Why.** The reason, including what was rejected.
**Consequences.** What must change as a result.
**Status.** active | superseded by <date>
```
