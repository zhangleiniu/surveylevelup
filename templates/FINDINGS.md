# Findings

Cross-paper findings: things that are true of the literature and that no single
paper states. This is the survey's differentiated content — the part a reader
could not get by reading the papers in sequence.

**Does not hold:** facts about one paper. Those are cards.

Start with the index. Each finding gets one line there and a short entry below.
Mark the load-bearing ones — the findings a section's argument rests on.

Every finding names its evidence and says what kind it is: observation across
papers, survey inference, or proposed mechanism. A finding whose evidence is "it
seemed that way while reading" is a hypothesis; label it as one or drop it.

## Reaction chains

Papers that correct, refute, re-evaluate or supersede one another. These are
usually the highest-value content a survey has and they rarely fall out of any
taxonomy, so they are tracked here explicitly.

If a project decides to formalize these, they move to `state/stances.jsonl` —
one row per `{citing, cited, stance, quote, page}`, extracted only for contested
pairs, never corpus-wide.
